1. Overview

KO CLIENT (“KO Client”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a desktop game client for Windows that connects players to DEADSHOT.io. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to provide optional account sign-in and deliver client updates. KO Client does not operate a central backend database to store your personal information, does not display third-party advertisements, and does not sell or rent user data.

2. Information We Access and Collect

Depending on how you use KO Client, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Google Account Data (Optional Google Sign-In): If you choose to sign in using Google OAuth 2.0, KO Client requests the standard non-sensitive scopes openid, profile, and email. This provides a Google ID token / access token containing your basic Google account identifier, display name, and email address.
  • Game Account & Session Data: When you sign in, KO Client exchanges your Google token with DEADSHOT.io’s authentication service (https://login.deadshot.io) to obtain a DEADSHOT.io session token (dses), load your in-game profile (username, clan tag, in-game currency balances, unlocked/equipped skins, and challenges), and sync equipped skins or matchmaking queue preferences when you change them.
  • Local Settings & Preferences: Graphics, audio, control bindings, crosshair preferences (settings.cfg), and your active game session token and device marker (account.cfg) are saved locally on your Windows device inside the application’s user data directory.
  • Update & Website Request Logs: When the KO Client auto-updater checks for updates or downloads asset packs from download.ko-client.online, or when you visit ko-client.online, our hosting and CDN providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) process standard HTTP metadata such as IP address, user-agent, and request timestamps to deliver files and prevent abuse.

3. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate you into your DEADSHOT.io game account when you initiate Google Sign-In.
  • Display your in-game profile, currency, challenges, and sync your equipped skin loadout with DEADSHOT.io.
  • Preserve your local session and client configuration across launches on your device.
  • Deliver software updates and modular game asset packs to your Windows client.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, or training artificial intelligence / machine learning models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

KO Client’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google OAuth tokens are obtained via RFC 8252 local loopback with PKCE directly on your device, used solely to authenticate your DEADSHOT.io game session, and are never transmitted to or stored on any KO Client server.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • Google LLC: Handles OAuth 2.0 authentication (accounts.google.com and oauth2.googleapis.com) when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • DEADSHOT.io: Receives your authentication token and gameplay traffic when you sign in and play matches on DEADSHOT.io servers.
  • Cloudflare & GitHub: Host this website (ko-client.online), client installers, and asset distribution (download.ko-client.online).
  • Discord (Website Only): On ko-client.online, your browser fetches public guild presence data from Discord’s widget API.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

All communications with Google OAuth endpoints, DEADSHOT.io authentication services, and KO Client update servers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. KO Client does not retain copies of your Google OAuth tokens, email address, or profile data on any remote server operated by us. Local session tokens and preferences remain on your device only until you sign out, clear the local configuration file, or uninstall KO Client.

7. Your Controls, Revocation, and Data Deletion

  • Sign Out in App: Signing out inside KO Client immediately clears your saved local session token and ends the active session.
  • Revoke Google Access: You may revoke KO Client’s access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions .
  • Delete Local Data: Uninstalling KO Client or deleting its local Windows user data folder removes all locally stored configuration and session files.
  • Support & Deletion Requests: Email us at support@ko-client.online.

8. Children’s Privacy

KO Client is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes & Contact

Updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Contact: support@ko-client.online.
