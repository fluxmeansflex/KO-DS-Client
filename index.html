Questions & Answers

Does it boost FPS on low-end laptops and PCs?

Yes. KO Client runs directly on Windows instead of inside Chrome, giving you higher FPS, less lag, and no browser ads.

Do I need to re-download for updates?

No. Download and extract the portable ZIP once (GitHub Release) and KO Client updates automatically when you open it.

What features are coming next?

Auto Highlight Capture, Match Replay, Aim Training, and Linux support are on the roadmap and coming soon.

Can I use my existing DEADSHOT.io account?

Yes. Log in with your DEADSHOT.io account as usual. All gun skins are unlocked inside KO Client.
